# Grapple Grab melee activation
Build 113 verified ordinary native melee can wake props. The A/B diagnostic menu is retired. DEV > Grapple Grab has two independent toggles: Grapple Grab (estimated ray anchor) and Melee Activation (session only, ordinary melee before pickup). Discovery and laser activate automatically. Grab and melee eligibility and the laser use a fixed 2 metres; legacy reach settings are ignored. Scan radius remains adjustable.
The existing left AIM override remains experimental; actual native melee direction is not yet verified. No saved weapon handedness or calibration changes. Logs retain MELEE-C and MELEE-PROBE for continuity.
Build 113 log: 39 grapple acquisitions, 103 rate-limited failed checks; all acquisitions were estimated ray proxies, not render mesh contacts. Largest sideways miss 54.46 game units (~1.38 m). Interim tolerance tightened from 55 to 25 units (~0.635 m at default scale) for selection and attachment. This may reject valid large props because their origins can be away from their visible surfaces. The existing estimated depth is not exact geometry. Verified render-mesh provider remains unresolved.
