# KHARVOX in-headset weapon-hand calibration (initial version)

This is an independent OpenXR compositor overlay in the **mod-enabled NativeBridge**, not a modification to the original DOOM or Cactus launcher's menus.

## Controls

- **Hold RIGHT thumbstick click for 1 second** during gameplay to open or close the VR calibration panel; another hold during calibration cancels and returns to the panel without saving. A normal short click remains DOOM world Use; it no longer toggles weapon-hand/head focus. Old LEFT Grip + BOTH sticks and RIGHT Grip + RIGHT stick capture shortcuts are gone.
- Point the **right controller** at a weapon, LEFT/RIGHT hand choice, CALIBRATE, or RESET THIS WEAPON. **Right trigger** selects the highlighted control. **Left thumbstick up/down** can scroll weapons; **right trigger** starts calibration when not pointing at a menu control. Only the explicit RESET THIS WEAPON button resets a profile.
- Equip the chosen weapon normally before starting calibration. The panel never cheats inventory or silently switches DOOM's selected weapon. A mismatch leaves the panel open with an explanation.
- For Gauss Cannon and Chaingun, choose **PROFILE: NORMAL** or **PROFILE: SIEGE/TURRET** in the same menu. These keep the separate alternate hand slots accessible without an old controller capture shortcut.
- On opening, KHARVOX detects whether the weapon was drawn into the **LEFT or RIGHT primary hand** (including Gunslinger hip holsters) and automatically selects that physical hand; the LEFT/RIGHT buttons can select the other hand for support-grip calibration.
- Selecting **CALIBRATE** hides the main panel. For **PRIMARY** weapon hand calibration, the actual primary-hand glove stays at the **physical weapon-hand controller GRIP pose** rather than being placed at the gun's AIM pivot or opposite controller position. With the primary weapon Grip **released**, moving/turning the opposite controller changes only the primary glove's saved visual **rotation**, preserving the gun and primary glove positions. The **SUPPORT** hand calibration still uses the opposite controller's live tracked three-dimensional placement.
- **Gun-only 1 cm fine-tune:** While calibrating the primary weapon hand, **hold the physical weapon-hand Grip**. The glove stays fixed to its actual controller; flick the **LEFT stick left/right** to slide the *gun* in X, **LEFT stick up/down** to slide the *gun* forward/back in Z, or **RIGHT stick up/down** to slide the *gun* up/down in Y. Each flick changes **only the gun's XYZ translation** relative to the controller-fixed glove, never its angle or the glove's position/rotation. Return the stick to centre before the next 1 cm step; haptics confirm accepted steps. Release Grip to adjust the glove rotation again. Gun-only offsets persist per weapon/physical hand/alternate mode in `_finetune_offset` fields; older saved profile files and backups are not deleted.
- Hold the **physical weapon-hand Grip** to test the position and use native alternate fire (Gauss Siege or Chaingun Turret) during live calibration; the other controller is free for fitting. Pull the **physical RIGHT trigger** once to save without firing and reopen the panel automatically. The saved XYZ offset persists per weapon, hand and alternate mode; the gun orientation always stays parallel to the controller AIM laser.
- The panel displays **LEFT**, **RIGHT**, **BOTH** or **-** to indicate which physical *weapon-holding* hand has a saved position and rotation. Support-hand positions do not falsely count as primary-hand calibration. RESET THIS WEAPON restores the exact uploaded factory poses and support placements for that weapon (including Siege/Turret), keeping other weapons unchanged and backing up the previous user calibration.

## Freely switch Grip-held gun position and Grip-released glove rotation

Primary weapon calibration has one unsaved draft throughout a session. Holding the physical weapon-hand Grip enables **GUN POSITION fine-tune**: 1 cm thumbstick flicks move the native gun relative to the physical controller-fixed hand and attachment anchor, leaving glove rotation unchanged. Releasing the weapon Grip returns immediately to **GLOVE ROTATION**: the gun remains at exactly the current local offset relative to the physical weapon hand. The first frame after release takes the opposite controller's rotation as a zero-delta reference, keeping the glove angle unchanged; further free-controller wrist movement changes ONLY the glove's visual rotation. Hold Grip again to continue moving the gun from the accumulated offset. You may repeat this cycle indefinitely without saving or recapturing position.

The runtime continues tracking the real weapon-hand controller throughout; "position held" means the gun's **offset relative to that controller**, not frozen at a world coordinate. RIGHT TRIGGER atomically saves both gun position and glove rotation; B / Back or a one-second RIGHT thumbstick hold cancels without saving either. Cancel leaves prior stored calibration untouched. On the next calibration session the saved pose and fine-tune offset are restored, rather than transient preview values.

## In-headset contextual calibration help

In PRIMARY calibration the translucent instruction panel follows physical weapon Grip state. With Grip **released**, it shows **HAND ROTATION / GRIP RELEASED** and explains how the opposite controller changes visual glove rotation. With Grip **held**, it shows **POSITION FINE-TUNE / GRIP HELD**, listing LEFT stick X = **gun left/right**, LEFT stick Z = **gun forward/back**, and RIGHT stick Y = **gun up/down**, one centimetre per deliberate stick flick. Tracking fallback and right-trigger SAVE / B CANCEL remain visible. The physical weapon glove never moves as a consequence of the fine-tune joystick; only the native weapon's positional offset changes. Support-hand capture prompts are unaffected.

## Support-hand preview and saved-position alignment

Support-hand calibration now samples the **physical free/support controller's GRIP tracking origin**, rather than the controller's AIM laser, so the LEFT (or RIGHT) support glove does not jump forward as soon as CALIBRATE is pressed. The preview is rejected if its physical GRIP pose is invalid; an unrelated valid AIM ray cannot silently write an offset in the wrong coordinate frame. Support hand rotation still follows the free physical controller while fitting; unlike the PRIMARY glove, its live 3D placement is unchanged.

Both the temporary support preview and the saved/reloaded two-hand support glove now use the **same unadjusted weapon AIM anchor** from which the support's weapon-local position was recorded. Previously its position was measured against that origin but rendered against the gun's additionally fine-tuned XYZ origin, so a saved hand appeared to jump backward/forward by the gun's offset. Existing support profile file formats, handedness mirroring, special Gauss Siege / Chaingun Turret positions, two-hand solver and primary fine-tune are preserved. If the primary gun offset is intentionally changed after calibrating its support handle, the support handle may need recalibration to match the weapon's new geometry.

## Controller-locked primary hand attachment anchor

The **physical weapon-hand controller's tracked GRIP pose** is the authoritative primary hand attachment/rotation anchor. This anchor and the rendered primary glove follow the real weapon-hand controller; the native weapon has a **separate, independently moving model origin**. Grip-held 1 cm XYZ fine-tune moves **only the gun relative to the fixed hand/anchor**, not the tracked glove. The gun's native orientation remains unchanged by fine-tune.

When both hands grip the weapon in normal gameplay, the existing support-hand orientation solver remains active. Its primary pivot now uses the **physical weapon-hand GRIP position**. Older support-grip offsets (originally stored relative to AIM) are converted to the controller-fixed pivot so their initial world-space pickup positions stay the same. The gun's original AIM-to-GRIP displacement rotates around the fixed grip anchor instead of dragging the attachment anchor along with the gun. Gun-only fine-tune still translates the gun relative to that anchor. This change is independent of saved calibration/weapon models, and does not reset any UserData.

## Normal gameplay two-hand weapon steering

**Visual weapon-hand lock during two-hand gameplay:** while a valid support-hand Grip is latched and the existing two-hand orientation solver is steering the weapon, the visible PRIMARY weapon glove now uses the weapon's final rendered orientation plus its own saved weapon-local wrist quaternion. The glove no longer appears to twist relative to or let the gun spin inside it. The weapon-hand controller remains physically unconstrained and fully tracked for gameplay and the solver; the controller-fixed GRIP position/attachment anchor is also unchanged. Releasing the support Grip immediately restores the ordinary single-hand visual frame. Calibration menus and Grip-held fine-tune still behave as before; only the glove's rendering changes during active normal two-handed gameplay.



When Weapon Grip Calibration and custom hand models are active in **normal gameplay**, the gun follows the primary controller's OpenXR AIM orientation until the support hand grips near its saved support position and the existing two-hand system actually latches. The primary-hand attachment pivot remains controller-fixed, not weapon-root-fixed. While held and tracked, **moving the support controller steers the gun** through the existing two-hand orientation solver; the attached support-hand mesh follows the same solved weapon pose. Releasing the support Grip, losing tracking, switching weapons or holstering returns to primary-controller AIM. The normal weapon-hand calibration overlay and the older two-hand calibration mode are deliberately excluded. Saved weapon-hand grip offsets, joystick fine-tune, gun/hand depth and gunstock settings are unchanged.

## Rotation behaviour

**PRIMARY weapon hand (LEFT or RIGHT):** the equipped gun follows the physical primary controller's AIM orientation when held one-handed, and the existing two-hand solver when a valid support grip is latched in normal gameplay. Its positional calibration is independent. The visible primary glove's **position** follows its own physical controller GRIP position, eliminating the artificial vertical discrepancy from weapon-local grip anchoring; its saved visual wrist rotation remains independently adjustable with the free controller. Fine-tune shifts the **weapon**, never the controller-aligned hand or the gun's quaternion. Left/right weapon and alternate-mode profiles remain independent.

**SUPPORT hand (opposite the weapon hand):** only the support position is saved. Its rotation is sampled anew on every normal grip. RIGHT support positions for LEFT-held weapons are independent of LEFT support positions for RIGHT-held weapons. Normal and Siege/Turret alternate profiles remain separately selectable.

## Settings and backups

With visible custom hands, the equipped weapon's aim is controlled by the primary controller's OpenXR AIM pose, or by the working two-hand orientation solver while the support Grip is actually latched in normal gameplay. Saved normal-position Y/Z adjustments and 1cm XYZ fine-tune translate **the native weapon** relative to the controller-fixed primary glove. The virtual weapon hand uses the physical primary GRIP tracking origin and remains stationary relative to that controller during all fine-tune steps; its stored per-weapon visual rotation still works separately. Fine-tune never changes gun quaternion, calibration/reset does not delete user profiles, and scene-depth/clipping, left-hand GLBs, Gunslinger, holsters and native launcher are unchanged.

The **editable** primary glove poses, rotations and separately persisted gun-only `_finetune_offset` values are stored at `UserData/offhand_right_hand_positions.cfg`. The right-primary support grips are in `UserData/two_hand_weapon_profiles_saved.cfg`; left-primary support grips are in `UserData/two_hand_weapon_profiles_left_primary_saved.cfg`. On a fresh install these are seeded from the owner's uploaded calibration files shipped read-only in `NativeBridge/CalibrationDefaults/`. Per-weapon Reset restores matching factory values without deleting other weapon calibrations, while writing `.before-vr-reset.bak` beside the active files. The updater preserves the entire `UserData` directory.

### Build/test limitations

Windows/GitHub builds and pure calibration policy tests can check compilation and serialization. Actual pointing, legibility, controller haptics and calibration alignment need Quest 3 headset verification. The menu supports weapons equipped in either physical hand and requires the existing Off-Hand Grip Capture mod to be enabled.
